PSL Fan Vote

Which team will lift the trophy? Vote on the blockchain!

This is a decentralized application (dApp) for MG3012 - Blockchain Technology for Business. Every wallet gets one vote, and all votes are stored in a smart contract on the Sepolia test network, so nobody can cheat or change them.

How to play

  1. Open MetaMask and select the Sepolia network.
  2. Click "Connect MetaMask".
  3. Press "Vote" next to your favourite team and confirm in MetaMask.
  4. Watch the live bars update. The leader gets a crown!
